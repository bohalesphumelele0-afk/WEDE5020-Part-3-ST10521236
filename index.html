Welcome to Ubuntu Youth Foundation

Since 2015, we have been dedicated to uplifting disadvantaged youth through mentorship, skills training, and community projects. Our mission is to empower young people with education and life skills, creating equal opportunities for all.

What We Do

Mentorship Programs

Connecting youth with experienced mentors who walk the journey with them.

Skills Training

Workshops in IT, entrepreneurship, and leadership that build real ability.

Community Projects

Initiatives to improve schools and local communities across Gauteng.
