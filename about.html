About Ubuntu Youth Foundation

Ten years of walking alongside young people in Gauteng.

Our Story

The Ubuntu Youth Foundation was established in 2015 to support disadvantaged youth in Gauteng through mentorship, skills training, and community projects. We believe in the African philosophy of Ubuntu — “I am because we are” — and strive to uplift communities by empowering young people.

Our work reaches learners between the ages of 15 and 25, together with the educators, sponsors and volunteers who make each program possible.

Our Mission

To empower young people with education and life skills, enabling them to reach their full potential and contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

A future where every youth has equal access to opportunities, resources, and mentorship, regardless of their background.

Meet Our Team

Our dedicated team of volunteers, educators, and community leaders work tirelessly to provide mentorship and training. Together, we create programs that inspire hope and build resilience.
