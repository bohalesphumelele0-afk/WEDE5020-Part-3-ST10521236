Our Programs

Practical skills, mentorship and opportunities for growth.

At Ubuntu Youth Foundation, we run a variety of programs designed to uplift and empower young people. Each initiative is tailored to provide practical skills, mentorship, and opportunities for growth.

Mentorship Program

We connect youth with experienced mentors who guide them in academics, career planning, and personal development. This one-on-one support helps build confidence and resilience.

Skills Training

Our workshops focus on IT, entrepreneurship, leadership, and communication. These sessions equip young people with practical skills to succeed in the modern workforce.

Community Projects

We organise initiatives to improve schools, libraries, and local facilities. These projects encourage youth to give back to their communities while learning teamwork and responsibility.

Sponsors and volunteers keep these programs running throughout the year.
